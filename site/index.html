About the group

Software runs everything. The hard question is no longer whether it works, but whether we can show that it does.

The is the research group of Prof. Lionel C. Briand at Lero, the Research Ireland Centre for Software, in the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems at the University of Limerick.

Our goal is to develop automated means of ensuring that software systems — including those built on AI — are reliable, secure and safe. That covers automated testing and analysis, fault localisation and repair, runtime monitoring, and the evaluation of the AI systems now being used to write software.

We care about solutions that survive contact with practice. Most of our projects begin with a problem brought by an industrial partner and are evaluated in that setting, so scalability and integration cost are treated as research constraints rather than as future work.

The group works closely with the Nanda Lab at the University of Ottawa, which Prof. Briand also directs.
